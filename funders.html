Likely Grant Funders for Icograma/IPAX (as a Nonprofit)

Based on the research, here's a breakdown of the most likely philanthropic foundation funders, mapped to IPAX's specific value dimensions.

IPAX sits at the intersection of digital accessibility research, open standards, public-interest technology tooling, and inclusive design infrastructure — a profile that maps to several distinct funder archetypes.

Tier 1: Highest Fit

Ford Foundation — Technology and Society Program. The clearest match. Ford already funds the W3C Web Accessibility Initiative directly — providing $660,037 for WCAG development through July 2026. IPAX extends that exact work by translating accessibility standards into design-native tooling. Ford's “Technology and Society” program explicitly funds infrastructure that makes digital systems more equitable and accessible. The pitch: IPAX is what happens downstream of WCAG and APCA — where standards meet real design practice.w3.org

Open Technology Fund (OTF) — FOSS Sustainability Fund. OTF's FOSS Sustainability Fund supports free and open-source software tools with public-interest missions, funded partly by Schmidt Futures and Omidyar Network. IPAX as an open-access toolset with no commercial lock-in is a near-perfect structural match. Grants typically range from $50K–$300K.duke.edu

National Science Foundation — Human-Centered Computing (HCC). NSF's HCC program explicitly funds accessibility tooling and research into “ability-based design,” including prior grants like $500K to UW for accessible mobile interfaces and $225K for visual impairment input tools. IPAX's grounding in the Barten/CIE visual sensitivity models and APCA perceptual research is academically rigorous enough to compete. A university partnership (e.g., with an HCI lab) would strengthen eligibility significantly.nsf.gov

Tier 2: Strong Strategic Fit

Mozilla Foundation — Open Web / Public Interest Technology. Mozilla has a long history of sponsoring accessibility work (GNOME/Orca screen reader grants) and broader open web infrastructure. IPAX's open-research posture and commitment to keeping tools freely accessible aligns with Mozilla's “open web for everyone” ethos. The Mozilla Technology Fund periodically runs open calls for public-interest internet tools.blog.mozilla.org

Schmidt Futures — Open Source Software Virtual Incubator / Plaintext Group. Schmidt Futures co-funded the OTF FOSS fund and has run open calls for OSS sustainability ideas ranging from $10K mini-grants to $100K+ awards. Their interest in “institution-building” for OSS ecosystems and open infrastructure aligns with IPAX as a research-backed design commons.opentech.fund

Omidyar Network — Open Internet / Responsible Technology. Omidyar co-funded the OTF FOSS Sustainability Fund and has consistently backed public-interest technology infrastructure. While their current focus skews toward governance and democratic tech, the “accessible digital infrastructure” framing of IPAX could fit under their responsible technology portfolio.

Tier 3: Thematic Fit, Longer Path

MacArthur Foundation — Technology in the Public Interest. MacArthur has funded public-interest technology broadly and has its own institutional commitment to inclusive web design. It's a slower-cycle funder and prefers established organizations, but a university or nonprofit fiscal sponsorship arrangement would open the door.macfound.org

Knight Foundation — Journalism / Civic Tech / Open Internet. Knight's News Challenge on “strengthening the Internet” funded 19 projects totaling $3.4M with an explicit “Access” category enabling broader internet participation. Knight has also funded accessibility for Open Data and cultural institutions. IPAX would need a strong civic-information or journalism framing (e.g., “accessible design standards for civic and news sites”) to compete.artsy.net

Consumer Technology Association (CTA) Foundation. CTA's foundation specifically funds technology projects improving lives of people with disabilities. Smaller grants but highly relevant mission alignment — and useful as a validation/credibility grant for a young nonprofit.cta.tech

Framing Analysis

The strongest cross-funder pitch combines three claims:

  • Infrastructure argument: IPAX is to WCAG/APCA what a developer framework is to a specification — it makes the standard usable in practice.
  • Systemic leverage: One toolset reaching thousands of designers has more aggregate accessibility impact than individual compliance audits.
  • EAA readiness: With the European Accessibility Act now in force, there's a time-bounded demand signal for compliance tools that foundations attuned to EU digital rights (especially Open Society Foundations) would recognize.opensocietyfoundations.org

A fiscal sponsorship arrangement through an organization like Numfocus, Code for Science & Society, or a UX/HCI-focused academic lab would solve the nonprofit structure requirement while Bustelo retains research leadership.
